You need to respond to a reasoning question by making logical inferences on a sequence of Scene-Graphs extracted from frames belonging to a video, called a Spatio-Temporal Scene Graph.

IMPORTANT CLARIFICATIONS:
- You will receive a question and a Spatio-Temporal Scene-Graph.
- Each Scene-Graph is a static symbolic representation of the scene in a frame.
- The Scene-Graphs are ordered chronologically.
- The Scene-Graph is represented as a set of tuples in the format: subject - relationship - object.
- The Spatio-Temporal Scene-Graph is a chronologically sorted list of Scene-Graphs.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Read the entire Spatio-Temporal Scene-Graph in chronological order and pay attention to the order of how events unfold.
2. Reason about the answer step by step.
3. After your reasoning, choose one of the alternatives and write it in a new line introduced by "A:".
4. Be careful to reproduce the chosen alternative exactly as it is presented.
